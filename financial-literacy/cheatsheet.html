📋 Formula Cheatsheet

Every key formula and table from this course, on one page for quick revision.

1 The Accounting Equation

Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity

Must hold true after every single recorded transaction. The entire flow: Journal → Ledger → Trial Balance → Financial Statements.

2 Liquidity Ratios

Current Ratio   = Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities        (ideal ≈ 2:1)
Quick Ratio     = (Current Assets − Inventory) ÷ Current Liabilities   (ideal ≈ 1:1)

3 Profitability Ratios

Gross Profit Ratio = Gross Profit ÷ Net Sales × 100
Net Profit Ratio   = Net Profit ÷ Net Sales × 100
Return on Investment (ROI) = Net Profit ÷ Capital Employed × 100

4 Solvency Ratio

Debt-Equity Ratio = Total Debt ÷ Shareholders' Equity

5 Marginal Costing & Break-Even

Contribution per unit = Selling Price per unit − Variable Cost per unit
Profit = Total Contribution − Fixed Cost

Break-Even Point (units)  = Fixed Cost ÷ Contribution per unit
P/V Ratio                 = Contribution ÷ Sales × 100
Break-Even Point (₹ sales) = Fixed Cost ÷ P/V Ratio

Sales for Target Profit (units) = (Fixed Cost + Target Profit) ÷ Contribution per unit
Margin of Safety                = Actual Sales − Break-even Sales

6 Budget Types, at a glance

TypeOne-line meaning
Fixed BudgetPrepared for one activity level; not adjusted later
Flexible BudgetPrepared for multiple activity levels; adjusts to actual output
Zero-Based BudgetBuilt from scratch each period; every cost re-justified
Master BudgetConsolidation of all functional budgets into one overall plan

7 Financial vs Cost vs Management Accounting — one-liners

BranchOne-line focus
Financial AccountingReports the WHOLE business's past performance to OUTSIDERS
Cost AccountingMeasures and controls the COST of a product/process
Management AccountingHelps managers PLAN and DECIDE, using any relevant data
