⚠️ Two small notes on the poster:
  • The box at the top right reads "Kubermetes" — it means Kubernetes.
  • kubectl get all doesn't really list every resource. It shows the common ones (pods, services, deployments, replicasets…) but skips ConfigMaps, Secrets, PVCs and Ingresses. Ask for those by name, e.g. kubectl get configmaps,secrets,pvc.
